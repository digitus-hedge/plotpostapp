Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

PlotPost helps people find and share property listings in Kerala. This policy explains what information PlotPost collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it. "We" means the PlotPost team.

1. Information we collect

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your password, which we store only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we can't read. If you continue with Google, Google gives us your name, email address, profile photo and a Google account ID.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: phone number, profile photo, bio, agency, role (for example "Owner" or "Real estate agent") and place.
  • Your listings: the property details, price, photos, the locality you pick, how buyers should contact you and the link to your original Instagram, Facebook or YouTube post.
  • What you do in PlotPost: listings you save or like, your comments, people you follow or block, reports you send, chat messages, and inquiries you send to sellers (the name, phone number, email and message you enter).
  • Phone alerts: if you allow notifications, a token from Firebase that lets us send alerts to your phone.
  • Listing statistics: when someone opens a listing or taps Call, Open link or Share, we note it once a day so the seller can see how many people looked. For logged-in people the note uses their account ID; for visitors who aren't logged in, it uses a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of their internet address. These notes are deleted after 2 days; only the totals stay.
  • Technical information: our server records internet (IP) addresses, what was requested and the type of device or browser, to keep PlotPost working and secure.

We don't collect your precise location, your contacts or your advertising ID, and PlotPost shows no ads. The app keeps your login, a few settings and (if you aren't logged in) the listings you save on your phone.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, log you in and confirm your email with a 6-digit code.
  • To show listings, profiles and comments, and to pass inquiries and chat messages between buyers and sellers.
  • To send the alerts and emails you've asked for: new inquiries, messages, comments and followers, reminders before a listing expires, and codes.
  • To keep PlotPost safe: blocking offensive words, handling reports within 24 hours, limiting repeated login and code attempts, and removing content or accounts that break the Terms.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Anyone, including visitors who aren't logged in: your listings (details, photos and locality), your public profile (name, photo, bio, agency, role and place) and your comments.
  • Logged-in users: also the phone number on your listings and profile. Phone numbers typed into listing text are hidden from visitors who aren't logged in.
  • The seller you contact: your inquiry, with the details you entered. If the seller has turned on inquiry emails, they also get a copy by email.
  • The people in a chat: that chat's messages.
  • The PlotPost team: account details, listings, comments and reports, so we can handle reports and run the service.

4. Services that help run PlotPost

These companies store or process information only to provide their service to us:

  • MongoDB Atlas: our database.
  • Railway: runs our server.
  • Cloudinary: stores and shows photos.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers phone alerts.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose Continue with Google.
  • An email delivery service such as Brevo: sends codes and inquiry emails.

Some of them keep information on servers outside India. We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect people from fraud or harm.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and activity: until you delete your account.
  • Listings: until you or the PlotPost team delete them. After 90 days a listing expires and is hidden from others until you renew it.
  • Codes sent by email: they stop working after 15 minutes.
  • Daily listing statistics: 2 days.
  • Reports, and records of actions the PlotPost team took (such as removing a listing): 12 months after the report is closed or the action is taken.
  • Server logs and backup copies: up to 30 days.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Delete Account.
  • On the web, without the app: the Delete account page. We email you a code to confirm it's you.

Deleting removes your account and profile, your listings and their photos, inquiries you sent or received, your chats (for both people in them), your comments, likes, follows, saved listings, notifications and phone-alert registrations. It happens straight away and can't be undone. Reports, the team's records, server logs and backups are kept for the times in section 5, then deleted.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile, and edit or delete your listings, at any time.
  • Turn phone alerts and inquiry emails on or off in Settings, or turn off notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Block anyone you don't want to hear from.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, using the contact details below.

8. Security

PlotPost uses HTTPS between the app and our server, stores passwords and email codes only in hashed form, and limits repeated login and code attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and never share your codes.

9. Children

PlotPost is meant for adults (18 and over). If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll also let you know in the app or by email.
