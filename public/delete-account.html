Delete your PlotPost account

You can delete your account here, without the app. In the app you can also do it from Profile → Settings (gear icon) → Delete Account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile
  • Your listings and their photos
  • Inquiries you sent, and inquiries you received for your listings
  • Your chats and their messages (for both people in them)
  • Your comments, likes, follows and saved listings
  • Your notifications and phone alerts

It happens straight away and can't be undone.

What we keep for a while

  • Reports made by you or about you, and records of actions the PlotPost team took: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Server logs and backup copies: up to 30 days.
